Intro to Chinese

中文入門

Chinese in five minutes

No alphabet, four tones, and characters that began as pictures more than 3,000 years ago. Here is everything you need before your first card.

The languageCharactersPinyinTones

1The language

Mandarin Chinese is spoken by more than a billion people. It is the main language of Taiwan, where it is called 國語 or 華語, and of mainland China, where it is called 普通话. It is also an official language of Singapore.

"Chinese" is really a family of languages: Mandarin, Cantonese, Taiwanese Hokkien and others sound very different but share one writing system. PinZi teaches Mandarin.

Good news: Chinese grammar is simple. No verb conjugations, no plurals, no genders. 我去、你去、他去 — "go" never changes.

2Characters began as pictures

→日rì · sun
→月yuè · moon
→山shān · mountain
→人rén · person
→木mù · tree
→口kǒu · mouth

The oldest Chinese writing, oracle bone script 甲骨文, was carved on bones and shells about 3,200 years ago. Over time the pictures became simpler lines:

  1. Oracle bones 甲骨文around 1200 BCE
  2. Seal script 篆書unified by the first emperor, 221 BCE
  3. Clerical script 隸書Han dynasty; curves became straight strokes
  4. Regular script 楷書settled by the Tang dynasty; still used today
  5. Simplified 简体字mainland China, from the 1950s; Taiwan keeps Traditional

Most characters are built from parts, which makes them easier than they look:

日 + 月 = 明sun + moon = bright人 + 木 = 休person by a tree = rest女 + 子 = 好woman + child = good女 + 馬 = 媽"woman" gives the meaning, 馬 mǎ gives the sound = mā, mom

That last kind, meaning part + sound part, is the most common type of character. Spot the parts and new characters get much easier.

3Pinyin: Chinese in Latin letters

Characters don't show how to say them, so learners use pinyin 拼音: Chinese sounds written in Latin letters, with marks for the tone. It was introduced in 1958 and is used worldwide. 你好 → nǐ hǎo.

Every syllable has a start sound, an end sound and a tone: m + a + tone 3 = mǎ. Watch out for letters that sound different from English: q ≈ "ch", x ≈ "sh", zh ≈ "j", c ≈ "ts".

In Taiwan, children learn Zhuyin 注音 ㄅㄆㄇㄈ instead of pinyin. You will see it in Taiwanese books and phone keyboards.

4The four tones

In Mandarin, the pitch of your voice changes the meaning. Same sound, different tone, different word. Tap each one to hear it:

These are your device's built-in voice, so they may sound a little robotic. Listen for the shape of the pitch.

Three tone changes to know

  • Two 3rd tones in a row: the first becomes 2nd. 你好 is written nǐ hǎo but said ní hǎo.
  • 不 bù becomes bú before a 4th tone: 不是 búshì.
  • 一 yī becomes yí before a 4th tone (一個 yíge) and yì before other tones (一天 yìtiān).
