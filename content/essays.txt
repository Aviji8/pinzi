院子裡有一個大箱子，櫃子裡放著舊書，我常忘記把書放回去。|There's a big box in the yard, and old books in the cabinet. I often forget to put books back.
冬天氣溫在零度以下的時候，我們就在家裡看書。|In winter, when it's below zero, we stay home and read.
## 51
上個月底我搬家了。|I moved at the end of last month.
新房子的房東很好，可是房屋很舊，屋子裡很亂。|The new landlord is nice, but the house is old and messy inside.
大門外面有一棵樹，樹底下開著幾朵花。|Outside the front door there's a tree, with a few flowers blooming under it.
夏天是颱風的季節，天氣很濕，蚊子也很多。|Summer is typhoon season; the weather is humid and there are many mosquitoes.
颱風來的時候，我的傘都壞了。|When the typhoon came, my umbrellas all broke.
房東說，大家要做環保，垃圾要分類，才不會汙染環境。|The landlord says we must be eco-friendly and sort garbage so we don't pollute the environment.
三天內，我把房子整理好了。附近有一條很深的河，晚上很涼，自然環境很美。|Within three days I tidied up the house. Nearby there's a deep river; nights are cool and nature is beautiful.
## 52
假日早上，鬧鐘一響我就起床。|On a day off, I get up as soon as the alarm rings.
我穿上拖鞋，拿毛巾、牙膏和肥皂去洗手間。別忘了衛生紙！|I put on slippers and take a towel, toothpaste and soap to the bathroom. Don't forget toilet paper!
然後我去公園慢跑，跑得一直流汗。|Then I go jogging in the park and sweat the whole time.
中午我和朋友去河邊野餐，我們用籃子帶食物。|At noon my friends and I have a picnic by the river; we carry food in a basket.
河水很淺，也流得很慢，小孩子在河裡抓魚。|The river is shallow and flows slowly; kids catch fish in it.
我的朋友是一個畫家，她拍了很多照片，回家要畫畫。|My friend is a painter; she took many photos to paint at home.
晚上我用刷子洗鞋，寫日記，然後把頭放在枕頭上，做了一個好夢。|In the evening I scrub my shoes with a brush, write in my diary, then lay my head on the pillow and have a good dream.
## 53
週末，我陪朋友去參加一個派對。|On the weekend I went to a party with a friend.
主人是我的老同學，她看起來很高興。|The host is my old classmate; she looked very happy.
我們一邊喝茶一邊聊，也玩遊戲、跳舞。|We chatted over tea and played games and danced.
派對的目的是讓大家認識新朋友。主人要求大家合作，一起做菜。|The party's purpose was to meet new friends. The host asked everyone to cooperate and cook together.
第二天，我跟一個女生約會，我們先去茶館，再去游泳池。|The next day I went on a date with a girl. We went to a teahouse first, then a swimming pool.
游泳池的門票只要一百塊，我們游了一個小時。|The swimming pool ticket was only 100 dollars, and we swam for an hour.
我利用週末休息，覺得很輕鬆。每件事都有好處和壞處，可是今天的情形都很好。|I used the weekend to rest and felt relaxed. Everything has pros and cons, but today everything went well.
## 54
春節是台灣最重要的節日。|Lunar New Year is Taiwan's most important holiday.
除夕晚上，全家人一塊吃年夜飯，大家同時說：「新年快樂！」|On New Year's Eve the whole family eats dinner together and everyone says at the same time, "Happy New Year!"
在這些傳統當中，我最喜歡吃年夜飯。|Of all these traditions, I like the New Year's Eve dinner most.
現代很多人用手機寄郵件，可是我寫了一封信，貼上郵票，放進信箱，祝朋友新年快樂。|Nowadays many people send email by phone, but I wrote a letter, put on a stamp, dropped it in the mailbox, and wished my friend a happy new year.
她給我回信說：「放心，我一切都很好。」|She wrote back, "Don't worry, everything is fine with me."
我出門的時候，給家人留了一張紙。新的一年帶來新的希望。|When I went out, I left my family a note. The new year brings new hope.
法律保護大家的安全，所以放煙火要小心。|The law protects everyone's safety, so be careful setting off fireworks.
## 55
我從美國飛到台灣，有十二個小時的時差。|I flew from the US to Taiwan; there's a twelve-hour time difference.
台北市區的交通很方便，我常搭捷運。|Getting around downtown Taipei is convenient; I often take the MRT.
在月台上，一位老太太對我說：「加油！你的中文很好。」她是在開玩笑嗎？|On the platform, an old lady said to me, "Keep it up! Your Chinese is good." Was she joking?
在台灣，到處都是機車。|In Taiwan, there are scooters everywhere.
週末我們坐巴士走公路去北投，巴士的速度很快，我們選的地點也很好。|On the weekend we took a bus along the highway to Beitou. The bus was fast, and the spot we chose was great.
北投的溫泉很有名，可惜那天人太多。|Beitou's hot springs are famous; too bad there were too many people that day.
我喜歡台灣社會的自由，在很多方面都很好。|I like the freedom of Taiwanese society; it's good in many ways.
## 56
我的朋友是一個有錢的商人，他做生意去過非洲、美洲和歐洲。|My friend is a rich businessman. He has been to Africa, the Americas and Europe on business.
今年他來亞洲旅行，先去中國大陸，再來台灣。|This year he traveled in Asia, first to mainland China, then Taiwan.
他說東方的文化很有意思。來台灣以前，他辦了簽證。|He says Eastern culture is fascinating. Before coming to Taiwan, he got a visa.
他背著一個大背包，背包上有一個鎖。|He carried a big backpack with a lock on it.
在博物館的入口，他拍照拍了很久，流了很多汗。|At the museum entrance he took photos for a long time and sweated a lot.
後來他的臉色不好，說他頭痛。休息一下以後，他的精神好多了，一切正常。|Later he looked pale and said he had a headache. After a rest, he felt much better and everything was normal.
他說台灣的服務標準很高。|He said Taiwan's service standards are high.
## 57
今天百貨公司打折，我跟朋友去逛街。|The department store had a sale today, so I went shopping with a friend.
我買了一件上衣、一雙皮鞋和一雙手套。店員說可以刷卡，我就刷卡了。|I bought a top, a pair of leather shoes and a pair of gloves. The clerk said I could pay by card, so I did.
我們提著很多東西去吃飯，可是餐廳客滿了，因為我們沒有訂位。|We carried lots of bags to dinner, but the restaurant was full because we hadn't booked a table.
下次要早一點訂。|Next time we need to book earlier.
晚上我們去逛夜市，老闆娘送我們一杯茶。|In the evening we went to the night market, and the lady boss gave us a cup of tea.
我在口袋裡找到一些零錢。在台灣，一般不用給小費。|I found some change in my pocket. In Taiwan you usually don't need to tip.
買到便宜的東西不困難，今天我得到了很多快樂。|It isn't hard to find cheap things; today I got a lot of joy.
## 58
我每個月存一點零用錢。|I save a little pocket money every month.
我的早餐很簡單：一個饅頭、一杯豆漿。|My breakfast is simple: a steamed bun and a cup of soy milk.
中午我吃一個熱狗或火腿三明治，喝可樂。|At noon I eat a hot dog or a ham sandwich and drink cola.
週末我去一家高級餐廳吃海鮮和沙拉。|On weekends I go to a fancy restaurant for seafood and salad.
我最喜歡的水果是芒果、葡萄和橘子。我買了一袋水果，可是袋子破了！|My favorite fruits are mango, grapes and tangerines. I bought a bag of fruit, but the bag broke!
回家以後，我喝了一杯烏龍茶，吃了豆腐。|At home I drank a cup of oolong tea and ate tofu.
我還買了一套新衣服，很適合我，大小也很合適。|I also bought a new outfit. It suits me, and the size fits well.
## 59
除了奶茶，我也喜歡喝汽水。|Besides milk tea, I also like soda.
我在汽水裡加了冰塊，喝了一罐。|I added ice cubes to my soda and drank a can.
夜市的臭豆腐聞起來很臭，可是吃起來香得不得了！|Night market stinky tofu smells awful, but it tastes amazing!
那家店的炒飯和炸雞也很好吃。不管多忙，我都會去吃一次。|That shop's fried rice and fried chicken are delicious too. No matter how busy I am, I go once.
我還買了一顆很熟的芒果當甜點。這樣的味道多麼好！|I also bought a ripe mango for dessert. What a wonderful taste!
朋友說：「你不必每次都吃一樣的，可以試試別的。」|My friend said, "You don't have to eat the same thing every time. Try something else."
我說：「我就喜歡那樣的味兒。」|I said, "I just like that kind of flavor."
## 60
我從來沒有在山上過夜。上個月我跟三個朋友去了，其中一個是我的同學。|I had never spent the night on a mountain. Last month I went with three friends; one of them is my classmate.
天氣相當好，尤其是早上，好極了。|The weather was quite good, especially the morning; it was excellent.
我們先爬山，接著做飯。|We hiked first, then cooked.
沒想到晚上下大雨，結果我們整個晚上都沒睡。|Unexpectedly it rained hard at night, and as a result we didn't sleep all night.
雨越下越大，我們立刻回到車上。|The rain got heavier and heavier, and we immediately went back to the car.
其實我完全不怕，只要有朋友在，一切都好。|Actually I wasn't scared at all; as long as my friends are there, everything is fine.
還好第二天早上雨已停了，我們大概十點就到家了。我正在想，下次最好先看天氣。|Luckily the rain had stopped the next morning, and we got home around ten. I'm thinking that next time I'd better check the weather first.
## 61
我第一次來台灣是五年前，當時我連一個中文字都不會。|The first time I came to Taiwan was five years ago; back then I couldn't even read one Chinese character.
「哇！這裡的一切都很新。」我說。那時我的房間很空，什麼家具都沒有。|"Wow! Everything here is new," I said. My room was empty then, with no furniture.
外國人來台灣須辦簽證，可是我的朋友剛好也住在附近，她幫了我很多。|Foreigners must get a visa to come to Taiwan, but luckily my friend lived nearby and helped me a lot.
我每天走一萬步左右，一步一步認識台北，生活所需的東西都很方便買到。|I walked about ten thousand steps every day, getting to know Taipei step by step; everything you need for daily life was easy to buy.
比方說，我學會了泡茶，也知道茶有很多類，包括烏龍茶和紅茶。每樣我都喜歡，且都不貴。|For example, I learned to brew tea and learned there are many kinds, including oolong and black tea. I like every kind, and they're all inexpensive.
有時候我很煩，就直接去找朋友，或者打電話給媽媽。另外，另一個朋友常常把照片傳給我。|When I felt annoyed, I went straight to my friends or called Mom. Also, another friend often sent me photos.
有空的時候，我同樣喜歡去爬山。等到我會說中文的時候，我的快樂多了兩倍。台灣人總是很友善。|When I have free time, I also like hiking. By the time I could speak Chinese, my happiness had doubled. Taiwanese people are always friendly.
